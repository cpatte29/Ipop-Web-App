Our Story

Our Story

Born from a love of popcorn and a passion for helping communities

iPOP Gourmet Popcorn started with a simple idea: fundraising shouldn't be complicated, stressful, or require upfront investment. We believed there had to be a better way for schools, sports teams, and community organizations to raise the funds they need.

After years of experience in the fundraising industry, we saw firsthand the challenges groups faced - from managing inventory to collecting money to delivering orders. We knew technology could solve these problems and make fundraising accessible to everyone.

Today, we're proud to serve thousands of organizations nationwide with our 100% online fundraising platform. Our gourmet popcorn practically sells itself, and our technology handles everything else - so you can focus on what matters most.
